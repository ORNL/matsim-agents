# Terms and Conditions

By registering for and submitting to the Matsim-Agents Materials Discovery
Challenge, you agree to the following terms.

## Eligibility & registration

- Participation is open subject to organiser approval of your registration.
- One account per participant or team. Creating multiple accounts to evade
  submission limits is prohibited.

## Submissions

- Submissions are limited to **3 per day**. The submission rate limit exists
  to preserve the integrity of the held-out (private) evaluation set.
- You may submit any subset of the tasks. Omitted tasks are excluded from your
  score, not penalised.

## Predictions must come from a model, not from DFT

- This is a machine-learning-potential benchmark. Predictions **must** be
  produced by your model.
- Submitting values obtained by running DFT (or any first-principles
  calculation) on the released geometries is **prohibited**.
- All submissions are automatically screened for accuracy that is physically
  implausible for an ML potential. Flagged submissions are subject to manual
  review and **may be disqualified** at the organisers' discretion.
- Attempting to reverse-engineer the hidden labels or the private/public split
  (e.g. by exhaustive leaderboard probing) is prohibited.

## Evaluation & ranking

- The live leaderboard reflects the **public** partition (~30 % of
  structures). The **final ranking** is determined on the **private**
  partition (~70 %), revealed when the competition closes.
- The organisers may correct scoring errors and re-run evaluations as needed.

## Data usage

- Provided data may be used for the purpose of participating in this
  competition. Redistribution of competition data is not permitted without the
  organisers' consent.

## Changes

- The organisers reserve the right to update these terms, the schedule, the
  scoring, and the prize/recognition structure. Material changes will be
  announced to participants.
