## 5. Scientific findings supported by the evidence

- `single_llm` and `multi_llm_no_critique` matched the reference on 23/23 cases; `multi_llm_cross_critique` matched 21/23, with errors on phosphorene supercell consistency and the Cantor ideal static-force claim. No arm outperformed `single_llm` on the automated metrics.
- **Cross-critique failure mechanism:** the two final-round verdicts split (contradicted vs unresolved) and the tied two-model aggregation returned `unresolved`, although the 1.0175 eV/atom unit-cell/supercell discrepancy decisively contradicts the claim.
- no_llm reached correct verdicts but missed justified follow-up DFT requests (it requests DFT only for unresolved thermodynamic-stability claims) and scores near zero on reasoning and uncertainty.
- LiFePO4: the archived calculation is ferromagnetic-initialized (16.0 muB); this contradicts the claim about that calculation, not a statement about the true ground state. Antisite ranking and hull claims remain unresolved (unconverged antisite; no competing-phase references).
- Phosphorene: vacancy formation remains unresolved because the pristine references are inconsistent.
- Nb-Ta-O: v1 formation-energy sign was correctly unresolved (no O2 reference in the frozen packet); v2, with qualified triplet O2, makes the uncorrected PBE elemental formation energy (-2.689 eV/atom) decidable. This is not hull or phase stability. v1 and v2 share Q1/Q2 evidence and are one dependency cluster.
- Zn-formate: alpha vs Pbcn differ by 0.037 meV/atom on different k-meshes; the sign is decidable for the recorded calculations only, not a polymorph stability ranking. The dihydrate is a different composition and is not compared energetically.
- BCC: the ideal configuration met its 0.01 eV/A force criterion (non-spin-polarized). The sampled-configuration ranking is unresolved: no k-point or spin-polarized sensitivity data exist.
- Cantor FCC: ideal and random1 static SCFs independently qualified with matching settings/PAWs, normal termination, finite force vectors, and static POSCAR hashes equal to the respective relaxation CONTCAR hashes. Their preceding ionic relaxations remain not qualified (five and four ionic steps hit NELM=200). Ideal static force is 0.012182 eV/A (supported); random1 static force is 0.465874 eV/A (contradicted). The sigma-zero static energies are -7.828097257 and -7.827904987 eV/atom, only 0.192270 meV/atom apart. Random1's fresh static solution has 63.7847 muB versus 62.2226 muB at relaxation end and a large residual force. The comparison is not a physically reliable relaxed/configurational or magnetic-state ranking. random2 was interrupted by PBS walltime at step 21 (0.169 eV/A, 9 ionic steps at NELM); random3 hit MPIR_CVAR_PROGRESS_TIMEOUT=600 during MPI_Reduce, then SIGTERM/exit 143 at step 6 (0.314 eV/A, 4/6 steps at NELM). Neither produced a static.
- Cantor arm results: no_llm, single_llm, and independent multi-LLM correctly classify all three claims. Cross-critique incorrectly labels the decidable ideal static-force claim unresolved, scoring 2/3. no_llm and cross-critique both omit the justified targeted DFT request for the unresolved ranking-adequacy case; single and independent multi-LLM request it.

## 8. Cantor FCC

Static protocol: VASP 6.6.1, spin-polarized PBE, Co/Cr_pv/Fe_pv/Mn_pv/Ni_pv, ENCUT 480 eV, Gamma 2x2x2, MP1 sigma 0.10 eV, NELM 200. Raw static OUTCAR/OSZICAR verification passed for ideal and random1; static POSCAR bytes match their preceding relaxation CONTCAR bytes.

| Exact configuration | Static SCF | Static Fmax (eV/A) | E0 sigma-zero (eV/atom) | Total moment (muB) | Force claim |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| ideal MATS-0080 | 64 iterations, < NELM | 0.012182 | -7.828097257 | 63.4263 | supported |
| random1 MATS-0081 | 105 iterations, < NELM | 0.465874 | -7.827904987 | 63.7847 | contradicted |

These are admissible static observables on the exact recorded geometries. They do not qualify the preceding relaxations. The ideal relaxation terminated normally and printed the ionic-accuracy marker with Fmax 0.012478 eV/A, but its per-ionic-step SCF failed at steps 2, 3, 4, 5, and 12 of 25 (NELM=200). Random1 likewise reached the marker and Fmax 0.017835 eV/A, but SCF failed at steps 4-7 of 37. Both relaxation qualifications therefore failed.

Random1 static started with a zero-byte WAVECAR and independently converged in 105 SCF iterations. Its force is 0.465874 eV/A and its final moment is 63.7847 muB, versus 62.2226 muB at relaxation end. The coordinates match exactly; the distinct moment is an electronic-solution sensitivity, not a geometry mismatch. The energy comparison is finite-mesh static ordering only: ideal minus random1 is -0.192270 meV/atom. Because both ionic relaxations failed full SCF qualification, random1 static is far above the force threshold, only one magnetic start and 2x2x2 k-mesh were used, and random2/random3 have no statics, the physically reliable relaxed-configuration ranking remains unresolved. The ranking case requests only targeted qualifying relaxations, matched denser-k statics, and one alternative collinear magnetic start if that ranking claim is retained.

Random2 reached 21 ionic steps and Fmax 0.169 eV/A when PBS walltime terminated the process; nine steps had hit NELM. Random3 stopped at step 6 with Fmax 0.314 eV/A and four of six steps at NELM; its log records MPIR_CVAR_PROGRESS_TIMEOUT=600 during MPI_Reduce, followed by SIGTERM and exit 143. Neither has a qualified static result.
