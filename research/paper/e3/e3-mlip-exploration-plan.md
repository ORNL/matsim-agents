# E3-MLIP benchmark protocol

This is the current E3-MLIP benchmark protocol. The matching executable
manifest is generated by `benchmarks.e3.production`; the E3-DFT/VASP
protocol, calculation IDs, and results are separate and unchanged.

## Benchmark question and scope

The benchmark records the candidate-generation, structural-coverage,
MACE-relaxation, convergence, and runtime outcomes of LLM-guided exploration
under a fixed resource budget. It is descriptive: one strategy and one seed per
material do not support a rule-based comparison, inferential confidence
intervals, or an independently validated discovery claim.

All six classes are included:

`bcc_refractory`, `fcc_cantor`, `lifepo4`, `phosphorene`, `nb_ta_o`, and
`zn_formate_mof`.

For each class, the run has three adaptive rounds and requests at most 64
unique geometry optimizations per round (192 per class; 1,152 total maximum).
The registered seed is `20261113`. A candidate is not replaced if generation,
deduplication, or screening leaves a round below 64 tasks. Each round enforces
at least one requested candidate in each configured size tier. One LLM call
per round selects from that material's allow-listed actions and may cite only
the initial design and that stream's previous-round evidence.

## Model, evidence, and optimization

Use the frozen MACE-OMAT-0 medium checkpoint, loader alias `medium-omat-0`,
SHA-256
`d4b14be9afa294eebdbe31a0280b26a0fa29715771e978cbfd3ca24e0d90307a`.
Workers verify the checkpoint before execution. Use the existing
material-specific action spaces and generators in
`benchmarks/e3/production.py`; generated structures must pass the shared
composition, periodicity, geometry, exact-hash, and symmetry-deduplication
checks. Zn-formate candidates must pass the MOF connectivity guard.

Geometry is the only acquisition evidence. Do not load DFT references, labels,
energies, forces, or results, and do not submit VASP. A timed-out geometry task
is terminal and recorded nonconverged; partial artifacts are preserved. No
final energy or convergence is fabricated. A 300-second timeout covers the
spawned task, including its initial model load and final measurements.

The optimizer uses the recorded MACE cell-relaxation protocol, including the
phosphorene cell mask, native stress, strain guards, and MOF connectivity
guard. The hard task deadline is parent-enforced, not the cooperative
between-step timeout. The exact execution policy and manifest hash are stored
with each stream, and cannot be changed on resume.

## Resource and stopping limits

Each class gets one Perlmutter allocation:

- Account: `m5216_g`; QOS: `gpu_premium`.
- 16 GPU nodes, four GPUs per node, 64 workers.
- Two-hour allocation limit per class.
- Three sequential rounds, each supervised by a 30-minute wall-clock limit.
- At most 64 generated/selected geometry tasks per round.
- At most five array elements run concurrently.

The maximum allocation is 192 GPU-node-hours (768 physical GPU-hours) over six
classes. The shared ledger reserves the full 768 GPU-hours before execution and
reconciles allocation time after each job. Premium queue billing multipliers
are separate from this physical GPU-hour cap; verify current project charges
before submission. Premium scheduling priority does not guarantee all six
array elements start together or finish at the same time.

The per-round supervisor includes generation, the LLM decision, and GPU
evaluation in its 30-minute wall clock. It kills the round process group at the
deadline and records an incomplete run. Consequently, 64 GPUs are available
for evaluation but continuous full utilization is not guaranteed. The two-hour
Slurm limit remains the final allocation boundary.

## Reporting and interpretation

Each material stream writes a Markdown `report.md`, a machine-readable
`campaign-summary.json`, round manifests/summaries, candidate structures, and
task-level trajectories and results. Report attempted, converged,
nonconverged, failed, and duplicate counts, plus measured runtime and GPU-hours.
Incomplete runs remain incomplete and may resume only with identical manifest,
model, code, seed, and execution policy.

Report per-material results. Do not pool unlike materials without an explicit
normalization. Do not call the lowest MACE energy a ground state, stability
result, DFT label, or independently validated discovery. Because the benchmark
uses only one seed and one strategy, report no strategy comparison or
confidence interval.
