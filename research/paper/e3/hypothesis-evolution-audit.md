# E3-MLIP hypothesis-evolution audit

**Scope and method.** This audit is a faithful record of the saved acquisition evidence, not a reconstruction of missing scientific hypotheses. It is a read-only audit of the six matched `e3-mlip-production` campaigns, seed `20261113` (model `gemini-4-carbon`). I examined the saved acquisition proposal JSONs, which contain each round’s prompt, raw model response/rationale, and accepted allocation; I did not rerun calculations or modify repository files. A proposal’s prompt provides cumulative, strategy-local action summaries. Reported per-action metrics include attempted/converged/nonconverged/failed counts, distinct final-geometry hashes, mean energy per atom, mean RMS displacement, GPU-hours, and runtime. They do **not** include ASE relaxation-step counts or forces. “Distinct final geometry” is not equivalent to “converged”: several nonconverged endpoints were distinct.

Round-1 prompts had no prior outcomes. Their rationales are starting allocation rationales, not formal scientific hypotheses. Across this cohort, no separate final LLM interpretation after the final executed rounds was found. All energetic interpretations below are MACE-only; no DFT labels or independent validation were supplied.

**Source convention:** each linked R1/R2/R3 proposal is `round-NN/acquisition/proposal-001.json` and is the authoritative prompt/response/allocation record. Run root: [matched six-campaign cohort](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production).

## 1. BCC refractory alloys

**Initial (R1).** The LLM proposed broad coverage, saying it would “capture diverse multicomponent local chemical environments and off-equilibrium strained configurations.” It sampled NbTaVZr, NbTaVHfZr, NbTaVHfZrTi, Nb2TaVHfZrTi, and NbTa2VHfZrTi; tiers 4/8/16/32/64 received 3/9/24/20/8 candidates. This is a sampling objective, not a claim about alloy stability or properties. [R1 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/bcc_refractory/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-01/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R1 → R2.** In the R2 prompt, cumulative R1 outcomes were 3/3 converged at tier 4, 7/9 at tier 8, 2/24 at tier 16, and 0/20 and 0/8 at tiers 32 and 64 (12/64 overall). The prompt also supplied action-level composition/perturbation outcomes: NbTaVZr converged in 3/3 tier-4 and 6/6 tier-8 candidates, with 0.468–0.599 Å RMS displacements; its tier-16 bounded-strain action converged 2/2 (0.67 Å). Larger Hf/Ti-containing cells were nonconvergent, generally with RMS displacement above 0.8 Å; NbTaVHfZrTi had 1/2 convergence at tier 8 under bounded strain. The model also noted NbTa2VHfZrTi’s lowest action-mean MACE energy, −9.738 eV/atom, but did not treat that as a reason to increase its sampling. It summarized this as a “sharp dependence of convergence on size tier, composition, and perturbation.” The revision is computational feasibility planning, not a material-level scientific conclusion. R2 allocated 17/32/13/1/1 by tier, putting 21 of its 32 tier-8 and all 13 tier-16 candidates on NbTaVZr, while maintaining minimum tier-32/64 probes and sampling other compositions mainly at tiers 4/8. [R2 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/bcc_refractory/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-02/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R2 → R3.** Cumulative prompt feedback was 20/20 at tier 4, 33/41 at tier 8, 11/37 at tier 16, and 0/21 and 0/9 at tiers 32/64 (64/128 overall). NbTaVZr led at tiers 8 (27/27) and 16 (11/17); other compositions remained less reliable, especially at larger sizes. The model said there was a “sharp size- and composition-dependent convergence boundary.” R3 allocated tiers 4/8/16/32/64 as 20/28/14/1/1. Tier 4 was spread evenly over all five compositions; tier 8 favored NbTaVZr (19) but included NbTaVHfZr and NbTaVHfZrTi; tier 16 was all NbTaVZr. The next-round allocation is consistent with the convergence evidence, although it is not evidence that NbTaVZr is physically more stable. [R3 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/bcc_refractory/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-03/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**Final assessment.** The final campaign summary reports 116/192 converged. The operational expectation that smaller NbTaVZr cells relax more reliably was supported in these runs; no initial material-science hypothesis was explicitly reassessed.

## 2. FCC Cantor alloy

**Initial (R1).** The model described broad sampling across equiatomic CrMnFeCoNi and three off-stoichiometric compositions, saying five-element chemical stoichiometry and configurational diversity should be “well resolved.” This is a coverage expectation, not a hypothesis about phase stability or composition-dependent properties. Tiers 4/8/16/32/64 received 4/8/16/20/16 candidates. [R1 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/fcc_cantor/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-01/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R1 → R2.** The R2 prompt reported 4/4 convergence at tier 4, 8/8 at tier 8, 14/16 at tier 16, 2/20 at tier 32, and 0/16 at tier 64 (28/64 overall). The two tier-32 convergences were bounded-strain actions. The action/formula summaries showed tier-16 convergence for the equiatomic, Mn-rich, and Fe-rich compositions at 4/4 each, but only 2/4 for Cr2MnFeCoNi; energies were reported as action means across the different compositions, not formation energies. The LLM’s “sharp size-tier convergence boundary” is an optimizer-feasibility statement. It allocated 37 candidates to tier 16 and 20 to tier 8 across all compositions and perturbations, 4 to tier 4, 2 to the two partially successful tier-32 strain actions, and 1 to tier 64. [R2 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/fcc_cantor/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-02/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R2 → R3.** Cumulative feedback was 8/8 at tier 4, 28/28 at tier 8, 46/53 at tier 16, 3/22 at tier 32, and 0/17 at tier 64 (85/128 overall). At tier 16, Cr2MnFeCoNi was 4/11, while CrMn2FeCoNi, CrMnFe2CoNi, and equiatomic CrMnFeCoNi each reached 14/14. R3’s rationale says the former “suffers elevated nonconvergence at tier 16.” The accepted allocation put 27 in tier 16 across the three fully convergent compositions, 30 in tier 8 (including 12 Cr2MnFeCoNi candidates), 4 in tier 4, 2 in tier 32, and 1 in tier 64. This is consistent as adaptive sampling; the prompt does not record a revised physical hypothesis about the alloy. [R3 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/fcc_cantor/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-03/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**Final assessment.** The campaign reports 145/192 converged. Low-tier convergence expectations were supported, and the poor large-tier results were repeatedly observed. The LLM did not explicitly reassess a scientific claim about Cantor-alloy thermodynamics. Per-atom energies from different stoichiometries cannot establish a composition ranking without a suitable reference/formation-energy analysis.

## 3. LiFePO₄

**Initial (R1).** The LLM said it would “establish baseline coverage across all four size tiers … and all three distinct structural families”: pristine, Li–Fe antisite pair, and bounded-strain olivine. It sampled tiers 4/8/16/32 with 12/14/18/20 candidates. This is an experimental-design plan, not a material hypothesis. [R1 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/lifepo4/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-01/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R1 → R2.** Every R1 optimization was nonconverged: 0/12, 0/14, 0/18, and 0/20 by tier. The prompt reported no failed runs and roughly 0.0856 GPU-hours per candidate across tiers. With no converged energy/RMS evidence to compare, the LLM described the result as “all prior … optimizations … terminated as nonconverged.” It nevertheless retained 6 tier-32 antisite-pair samples for “structural defect diversity” and allocated the other 58 to bounded-strain olivine: 15/15/14/14 across tiers 4/8/16/32. This is entirely computational planning. [R2 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/lifepo4/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-02/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R2 → R3.** The cumulative prompt distinguished families sharply: bounded-strain olivine converged 15/24 at tier 4, 15/24 at tier 8, 14/23 at tier 16, and 0/23 at tier 32; antisite actions remained 0-converged in all tiers. Overall cumulative tier results were 15/27, 15/29, 14/32, and 0/40 (44/128 total). Converged strain action means were approximately −6.795 eV/atom; the prompt also supplied RMS and GPU/runtime values. The model explicitly identified “100% convergence” for the productive strain actions. R3 shifted almost entirely to that family: 21 each at tiers 4/8/16 and the required minimum of 1 at tier 32; no antisite allocation. [R3 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/lifepo4/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-03/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**Final assessment.** The final campaign reports 107/192 converged. The expectation that the productive smaller-tier bounded-strain actions would yield converged relaxations was supported; the tier-32 exclusion/minimum probe was also borne out. No scientific hypothesis about LiFePO₄ defects, phase stability, or electrochemistry was explicitly reassessed.

## 4. Phosphorene

**Initial (R1).** The rationale was to “empirically evaluate post-relaxation survival and symmetry-aware deduplication yields across perturbations and supercell sizes,” with 32 candidates each for black and blue phosphorene over tiers 4/16/36/64/144. This is a computational test objective, not an initial claim that one allotrope is more stable. [R1 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/phosphorene/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-01/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R1 → R2.** The prompt gave 60/64 converged overall: 8/8, 14/14, 14/14, 14/14, and 10/14 by tier. At tier 144, black reconstruction was 0/3 and blue reconstruction 2/3, while the other summarized actions converged. It also supplied per-action energy means and displacements: blue phosphorene around −5.376 eV/atom; black reconstruction around −5.309 to −5.316, versus −5.259 for strained black. These are action-level MACE values, not DFT formation energies. The model’s genuine material-level interpretation was that blue’s lower MACE energy and black reconstruction’s lower-energy relaxed basins were worth prioritizing, while excluding tier-144 reconstruction after nonconvergence. It allocated 6/19/18/14/7 by tier, emphasizing blue and black reconstruction at tiers 16/36/64 and strain diversity. This is a model-predicted energy ordering, not proof of relative thermodynamic stability. [R2 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/phosphorene/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-02/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R2 → R3.** The cumulative prompt reported 14/14, 33/33, 32/32, 28/28, and 17/21 by tier (124/128 overall). The four nonconvergences in the cumulative feedback were still confined to tier-144 reconstruction. The model retained its hierarchy—blue near −5.376 eV/atom and black reconstruction near −5.310 to −5.312, below strained black near −5.259—and favored 100%-convergent actions. The accepted R3 plan was 6/16/16/16/10 by tier. **It was not executed:** generation stopped after eight unique partial structures; zero R3 optimizations ran and no post-round LLM update was made. This is confirmed by the [phosphorene campaign summary](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/phosphorene/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/campaign-summary.json) and [R3 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/phosphorene/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-03/acquisition/proposal-001.json).

**Final assessment.** The two executed rounds produced 124/128 converged structures. The LLM generated an explicit, refined MACE-energy interpretation, but it was not independently validated or reassessed after round 3. Relative stability remains unresolved; MLIP convergence and relative action-mean energies do not establish thermodynamic stability or DFT accuracy.

## 5. Nb–Ta–O

**Initial (R1).** The LLM described a “balanced Latin-like space-filling design” across eight oxide compositions, tiers 2/4/8/16/32, AFLOW/PyXtal prototypes and charge-compensated vacancies, strain, and symmetry families. That is a sampling design, not a scientific hypothesis. [R1 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/nb_ta_o/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-01/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R1 → R2.** R1 prompt feedback was 7/12 at tier 2, 6/12 at tier 4, 3/14 at tier 8, and 0/12 and 0/14 at tiers 16/32 (16/64 overall). Action summaries included examples of high-symmetry AFLOW/PyXtal prototype and vacancy success at tiers 2/4/8, with small RMS for some actions, but also very large RMS (up to 5.593 Å) and strong action/composition variation. The R2 rationale says “all prior tier 16 and tier 32 actions failed to converge (0/24)”; this does not match the same prompt’s tier totals, which sum to **0/26**. It allocated 58/64 to tiers 2/4/8 (18/24/16), with just 4/2 at tiers 16/32, favoring selected high-symmetry AFLOW/PyXtal prototype and vacancy actions. This is a computational feasibility revision. [R2 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/nb_ta_o/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-02/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R2 → R3.** Cumulative tier feedback was 21/30, 23/36, 10/30, 1/16, and 2/16 (57/128 overall). Outcomes varied materially by composition/action: for example, NbO₂ was 9/11 at tier 4 and 3/5 at tier 8, but mixed NbTaO₄ was 1/2 and 1/4 at those tiers; many large or strained/low-symmetry actions were nonconvergent and had large RMS. The LLM’s R3 claim that unstrained high-symmetry AFLOW prototypes/vacancies “achieve consistently high convergence … across all size tiers” is too broad for the supplied aggregate evidence: tier-wide results were 10/30 at tier 8, 1/16 at tier 16, and 2/16 at tier 32; for high-symmetry unstrained AFLOW prototypes specifically, the prompt rows give 7/15 at tier 8, 1/4 at tier 16, and 2/2 at tier 32. The two tier-32 observations are too few to establish consistent high convergence across all tiers. R3 allocated 22/20/13/5/4 by tier, concentrating on unstrained high-symmetry AFLOW prototypes for NbO₂, TaO₂, and NbTaO₄, with some oxygen-vacancy and pentoxide exploration. This follows the model’s action-specific planning direction but overstates its evidence in the generalization. [R3 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/nb_ta_o/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-03/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**Final assessment.** The completed campaign reports 103/192 converged. The adaptive computational strategy is documented; no final scientific interpretation of oxide stability or composition ranking was recorded. The R3 across-all-sizes convergence language is unsupported by the prompt’s tier totals.

## 6. Zn-formate MOF (matched production cohort)

**Initial (R1).** The rationale established “a balanced initial baseline” of 16 candidates at each of tiers 24/48/96/192 atoms, spanning bounded strain, connectivity-preserving distortion, and proton orientation. This is an operational baseline, not a scientific hypothesis. [R1 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/zn_formate_mof/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-01/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R1 → R2.** The prompt recorded zero converged and zero failed trajectories at every tier; all 64 were nonconverged. Yet there were distinct final-geometry hashes: 16/16 at tiers 24 and 48, 16/16 at 96, and 12/16 at 192. It also supplied action-mean energies (roughly −6.068 eV/atom at tiers 24/48, −6.067 at 96, and −6.066 to −6.067 at 192), RMS displacement (about 0.388–0.536 Å at 24/48 versus 0.810–0.873 Å at 192), and compute costs. The model explicitly used uniqueness, energy, displacement, and GPU cost as selection criteria—not convergence or evidence of stability. R2 allocated tiers 24/48/96/192 as 22/20/16/6, keeping a small tier-192 sample and favoring connectivity-preserving distortion over proton orientation. [R2 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/zn_formate_mof/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-02/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**R2 → R3.** Cumulative prompt feedback remained 0/38, 0/36, 0/32, and 0/22 converged by tier (0/128 overall). Distinct hashes were 38/38, 36/36, 32/32, and 18/22. Mean energies and RMS remained slightly lower for tiers 24–96 (−6.068 to −6.067 eV/atom; 0.41–0.70 Å) than tier 192 (−6.067 to −6.066; 0.80–0.94 Å). The LLM’s computational hypothesis—that smaller tiers give more unique endpoints at lower cost/displacement—was consistent with these summaries. It allocated 24/20/16/4 by tier, preserving a small tier-192 probe. No scientific claim about MOF stability was made. [R3 proposal](../../../runs/e3-mlip-production/zn_formate_mof/llm_guided_adaptation/seed-20261113/round-03/acquisition/proposal-001.json)

**Final assessment.** The matched campaign reports **0/192 converged**. This cohort’s saved outputs establish optimizer nonconvergence under its configured run limits, not absence of a physically stable MOF. A separate Zn-formate follow-up, `e3-mlip-zn-formate-10000` (different run and longer 1800-second optimization limit versus 300 seconds in this production cohort), reports 138/192 converged. It is not part of this six-campaign cohort and must not be used to overwrite its feedback or result.

## Three illustrative examples and provenance assessment

The logs do **not** support three genuine material-science hypothesis revisions. The clearest genuine case is phosphorene; two of the clearest *adaptive but operational* cases are included below rather than inventing scientific interpretations:

1. **Phosphorene — actual MLIP-energy interpretation.** Verbatim: “Allocations emphasize blue phosphorene (lowest MACE energies around -5.376 eV/atom) and black phosphorene reconstruction … around -5.310 to -5.316 eV/atom … compared to -5.259 eV/atom for strained black phosphorene.” The prompt’s tier-144 reconstruction evidence was black 0/3 and blue 2/3, while all actions at tiers 4/16/36/64 converged. This is a real change from broad perturbation testing to a model-based energy ranking and targeted allocation. It remains an MACE prediction, not a stability result.
2. **FCC Cantor — clear revision, but convergence-only.** Verbatim: tier 8/16 were productive while “Cr2MnFeCoNi suffers elevated nonconvergence at tier 16 (only 3/11 converged…)”. The exact cumulative prompt totals were 4/11 for Cr2MnFeCoNi at tier 16 versus 14/14 for each of the other three compositions. R3 avoided that composition/tier combination and upweighted Cr2MnFeCoNi at tier 8. This is sound adaptive sampling but not a scientific alloy-property hypothesis.
3. **LiFePO₄ — clear family/tier revision, but convergence-only.** Verbatim: “100% convergence … for bounded-strain olivine actions” at tiers 4 (15/15 in the R2 batch), 8 (15/15), and 16 (14/14), while tier 32 “failed to converge across both prior batches (0/9 and 0/14).” The cumulative prompt gives family totals 15/24, 15/24, 14/23, and 0/23 across tiers 4/8/16/32. The allocation moved to 21 each for tiers 4/8/16 and one tier-32 probe, entirely abandoning the antisite family. Again, this concerns optimizer behavior, not LiFePO₄ defect energetics or electrochemistry.

**Provenance limits and unsupported claims**

- The six campaigns share the matched production root and seed `20261113`; each R1 prompt had an empty prior-outcomes list. Initial rationales are therefore the only saved “initial” LLM statements, and they mostly describe design/coverage rather than falsifiable science.
- Prompts contain cumulative strategy-local **action summaries**, not raw optimizer traces. They do not show per-structure ASE step counts, forces, or a full per-candidate energy ranking. Use the actual prompt payload, not a retrospective reconstruction, when claiming what the model saw.
- Energies are action-level mean energies per atom. Cross-composition values are not formation energies; convergence, a low action mean, or distinct hashes do not establish thermodynamic stability, discovery, or DFT-level accuracy.
- Phosphorene’s R3 allocation is a proposal only. The campaign summary records generation exhaustion, eight partial candidates, zero optimizations, and no hypothesis update in the stopped round. Do not report the planned 64 candidates as executed.
