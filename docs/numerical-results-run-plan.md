The six target systems are LiFePO4, Cantor FCC alloy, NbTaVHfZrTi BCC alloy, phosphorene, Zn(HCOO)2 MOF, and Nb-Ta-O. ## Decision Summary

The evaluation shall include **all six material systems** across E1, E2, and E3: LiFePO4, Cantor FCC alloy, NbTaVHfZrTi BCC alloy, phosphorene, Zn(HCOO)2 metal–organic framework, and Nb-Ta-O. Nb-Ta-O E3 is intended to assess candidate phases against a bounded, explicitly versioned set of elemental, binary, and ternary references. Recovered historical labels may be reused only after the audit gates below; missing structures, phases, labels, or independent evaluation data must be generated anew. Candidate generation will use the internal AFLOW-prototype plus pyXtal randomized-structure mechanisms. Missing evidence must be reported, not replaced by synthetic labels or silently excluded. E3 model coverage remains conditional on backend support and valid independent evidence.

**E3 is one conditional, end-to-end experiment, not a two-material policy experiment followed by an unrelated fine-tuning study.** Build a method-compatible reference dataset for all six systems; for Nb-Ta-O, audit and conditionally reuse compatible NbO2 labels, then acquire missing target/reference and independent-test evidence. Evaluate pretrained HydraGNN, UMA, and MACE on every supported system–model combination (up to 18). Using predeclared, *validation-only*, task-specific acceptance criteria, identify every supported combination with insufficient predictive accuracy. Retain pretrained models that pass. For **every failing supported combination**, execute the unified E3 adaptive workflow: matched-budget comparison of LLM-guided policy C against deterministic policy D, conditional sequential DFT acquisition, and fine-tuning only when enough independent training data exist and adaptation is justified. Evaluate resulting models on independent test data and assess whether the scientific characterization requirements are met. Do not select failing combinations from final test results. A model incapable of serving a system is explicitly 'unsupported', not a passing or failing accuracy result. Eighteen initial evaluations do not imply eighteen fine-tuning campaigns.

Distinguish (i) inadequate MLIP predictive accuracy, which can motivate adaptation, from (ii) insufficient scientific evidence, which can require DFT even when the MLIP passes accuracy checks. Both decisions are governed by predefined contracts and do not authorize unsupported hull-stability claims. For Nb-Ta-O, generate all candidate structures, reference phases, labels, and validation/test evidence anew. Generate and validate only evidence required for six-system E2 and E3 endpoints. Any irreducible coverage gaps must narrow manuscript claims explicitly.

Use a staged sequence. Freeze the scientific protocols and reference methods, then generate the six-system DFT evidence required under explicit, approved resource limits. E1 can proceed without DFT; E2 uses independently reviewed evidence, and E3 establishes leakage-aware reference splits before evaluating up to 18 supported MLIP–system combinations. Qualify the numerical pathway on one or two representative cases, **but do not treat this small engineering qualification as the scientific scope of E3**. Authorize matched C-versus-D adaptive execution only for supported combinations that fail prespecified accuracy criteria, subject to a prespecified budget and stopping rule. E4 remains a single representative scientific workload qualified across three facilities, not a six-system × three-facility Cartesian product.

Retained Si/SiC characterization remains engineering evidence only. Recovered Nb-Ta-O artifacts are not campaign outputs; reuse is limited to records that pass the audit and declared-use checks, and they cannot substitute for missing hull references or independent test groups. Manuscript-era five-system datasets, warm-start outcomes, and aggregate totals are excluded from this campaign's baseline unless their archives are recovered and pass a separately documented provenance, method-compatibility, split-leakage, and accounting review. Do not present historical results as outputs of the new campaign. No pilot or production jobs are authorized by this plan.

### Nb-Ta-O Audit and Proposed Bounded Domain

**Historical run and reuse decision.** The run at `/global/cfs/cdirs/m5216/mlupopa/runs/portability/nb-ta-o--7llm-uma-al-qe-hull--653eb06--j59874625` used QE 7.6-dev, PBE, Nb/O pseudopotentials, 80/640 Ry cutoffs, Gaussian smearing, and four A100 GPUs per one-node QE child. The dataset manifests declare backend `qe`, method signature `qe-9b7290c69805cc1a`, raw QE total energy, and SHA-256 hashes for training and validation files. Eight training and two validation frames carry energies and forces. These are eligible *candidate* MLIP labels after file/hash, settings, pseudopotential, convergence, and lineage verification. They are not ten independent phase relaxations and cannot be used as hull energies. The validation frames are not accepted as an independent test set until structural-family and trajectory leakage are ruled out. The reference manifest lists structures for Nb, Ta, O2 and eleven competing formulas, but no computed compatible reference energies or convergence records; none of those entries currently qualifies as a hull reference.

**Target proposal for author review.** Make NbTaO4 the primary target composition, represented as the x=0.5 point of the rutile-derived `(Nb,Ta)O2` solid-solution line. Compare rutile-related and CaCl2-related parent structures and a small set of symmetry-distinct Nb/Ta orderings. Do not assume an ordered NbTaO4 line compound: report ordering energy relative to the lowest disordered/supercell approximant, and if no ordering is resolved call it a solid-solution composition, not a discovered ordered phase. Keep NbTaO5 as a secondary, explicitly lower-confidence block/T-Nb2O5-derived candidate. It is exploratory and must not become a required stable phase solely because an LLM proposed it. Exclude reduced shear formulas such as Nb12O29 from mandatory scope until a crystallographic source and formula/prototype mapping are independently validated; treat them as a later scope expansion.

**Minimum declared competitor domain (proposal, not exhaustive chemistry).** Include elemental Nb, Ta, O2; the Nb-Ta binary at NbTa plus elemental endpoints; Nb-O formulas NbO, NbO2, Nb2O3, Nb2O5; Ta-O formulas TaO, TaO2, Ta2O3, Ta2O5; and ternary target compositions NbTaO4 and NbTaO5. For each formula, freeze explicit phase IDs before calculation: curated structures where available, at least two distinct plausible polymorphs for NbO2, Nb2O5, TaO2, and Ta2O5, and the declared AFLOW/order variants for the ternary targets. Other formulas require at least one validated candidate phase ID. A formula is a composition requirement, not evidence that one prototype exhausts its polymorphs. This scope supports only the manuscript wording “stability relative to the declared DFT reference domain” or “domain-qualified convex-hull assessment.” List omitted ternary compositions, disordered configurations, high-pressure phases, and finite-temperature effects as out of domain.

**Three independent gates.** (1) Formula gate: the actual composition parsed from each structure matches its declared formula/phase record, and every required formula/subsystem is represented. (2) Polymorph gate: every predeclared phase ID and minimum polymorph family is present, hash-stable, and evaluated; store missing or undersampled families separately from formula gaps. (3) Convergence/compatibility gate: each required energy is from a converged geometry relaxation under the frozen method signature, backend, pseudopotential/correction policy, and convergence tolerances; SCF convergence or `JOB DONE` alone is not geometry convergence. Set `domain_qualified=true` only when all three gates and elemental references pass. Otherwise set `provisional=true`, `domain_qualified=false`, and expose the exact missing formula IDs, polymorph IDs/groups, and unconverged/incompatible records. A qualified hull remains relative to the declared domain, not a global completeness claim.

**E1-E3 roles remain distinct.** E1 proposes targets and tests (including the solid-solution alternative), without treating model consensus as evidence. E2 interprets a frozen, audited evidence packet and must identify historical versus newly generated records. E3 evaluates supported pretrained MLIPs, applies the predeclared accuracy gate, performs matched C-versus-D adaptive acquisition for failing cells, and independently completes the DFT evidence required for the Nb-Ta-O domain hull even if an MLIP passes. A hull requirement is not an excuse to adapt a passing model; a passing model is not a substitute for missing converged references.

**Incremental cost estimate.** The completed QE iteration reports 10 attempted/converged label jobs and 0.4516 node-hours for the DFT stage, equivalent to about 1.81 A100 GPU-hours at four GPUs/node, or 0.045 node-hours (0.181 GPU-hours) per label on that workload. Treat this as a broad qualification target, not a funded estimate: actual ionic-step counts, cell sizes, convergence failures, and resource concurrency are unknown. Use one-node/four-GPU measurements on the selected hull protocol to revise the cap before production. The historical six-hour 16-node parent allocations are dominated by campaign-level work and are not marginal DFT costs. Monetary cost is unknown without a verified facility billing rate.

**Required software changes for a later implementation review (not implemented here).** Extend `ReferenceCompletenessPolicy` with required phase IDs and polymorph-family/minimum-count requirements; extend its report with separate formula, polymorph, and convergence/compatibility gaps plus `domain_qualified`. Keep `provisional` true whenever any declared requirement fails. Validate formula correctness before hull construction, and require convergence independently of SCF success. ## Proposed Execution Stages
### Stage 0: Freeze the scientific design
1. Define the six-system candidate structure sets, scientific questions, independent grouping, required observables, reference methods, convergence criteria, and intended use in E2/E3. For Nb-Ta-O, review the audited reuse boundary, then freeze the target and competing-phase composition domain, AFLOW/pyXtal generator settings and random seeds, formula/polymorph/convergence gates, and domain-relative hull claim.

2. Freeze input-generation methods, structure/configuration hashing, software and pseudopotential versions, data schema, train/validation/test grouping rules, and handling of failed calculations.

3. Specify the initial bounded engineering/timing qualification, per-facility allocation caps, and hard stop/expand rules. Existing qualification artifacts may guide software setup only and must be labeled engineering-only.

4. Create the run manifest and accounting schema so every new request, DFT attempt, result, failure/retry, policy attribution, and scheduler parent/child allocation is captured from the first run.

**Gate:** no scientific DFT or pilot submission until the design, facility/account, bounded resource request, and explicit authorization are approved.

### Stage 1: E1 preparation and fresh E2 evidence generation

- E1: prespecify at least one substantive scientific question for **each of six systems**. The paired single/independent/debate experiment can proceed without DFT once model access, prompts, scoring, and inference-resource authorization are settled. The initial six-objective × three-block design is feasibility-oriented, not a power claim.
- E2: assemble independently reviewed, method-compatible evidence for all six systems, generating all Nb-Ta-O structures and DFT labels anew. Freeze evidence packets and gold answers before comparison, then give C and D identical packets and score raw proposed decisions separately from outcomes accepted by the common validator. Include Nb-Ta-O cases with incomplete reference information only with explicit uncertainty/abstention criteria.
- Report actual independent case count per class. Correlated structures, repeated prompts, and multiple LLM outputs do not become independent scientific cases.

**Gate:** do not start E2 assessment until each included case has a complete, method-compatible, reviewed reference packet; report any class gap rather than silently substituting another class.

### Stage 3: E3 six-system pretrained evaluation and adaptation eligibility

1. Audit and register compatible historical NbO2 labels; generate missing Nb-Ta-O candidate structures internally from AFLOW prototypes plus pyXtal randomized structures for every frozen composition, with reproducible seeds, source metadata, structure hashes, and deduplication. Compute missing elemental, binary, and ternary competing-phase references under the frozen method. Establish leakage-safe train/validation/test groups before model comparisons and hull reporting.
2. Evaluate pretrained HydraGNN, UMA and MACE in every **supported** system–MLIP combination (maximum 18). Record backend support and evidence limitations separately.
3. Apply prespecified, scientific-task-specific **validation** accuracy requirements. Freeze the `pass/fail/insufficient_data/unsupported` matrix. Preserve the independent test partition for outcome reporting, not for deciding which cell to adapt.
4. Estimate new acquisition costs for **every failing cell** from the approved fresh timing distributions; record separately which valid scientific claims require additional DFT even if the surrogate passes accuracy requirements.
5. Qualify the end-to-end implementation and cost extraction on one or two representative combinations. This qualification is a scheduler/software check and **cannot** substitute for experiments on the other systems.

**Gate:** authorize Stage 4 for each supported, failing cell only after independent evaluation evidence, matching DFT methods, safe data splits and cost ceilings are established.

### Stage 4: E3 conditional active learning, matched policies and model adaptation

- Apply the **same conditional E3 protocol to every failing supported model–material cell**, not an author-selected two-material subset.
- For each cell, compare C and D using the same initial model, candidate universe, evidence contract, data access and attempted-label resource budget; log all interventions, hypotheses, failure states and scientific resolutions.
- There is no offline label replay. For each failing cell, acquire new labels by approved DFT execution, preserving sequential policy decisions and blinded future outcomes. Deduplicate identical physical structure+method requests, but preserve each policy's request and cost attribution.
- Make fine-tuning part of the same adaptive workflow whenever data sufficiency and validation-based eligibility require it. Both arms must use the same training algorithm and model-promotion safeguards. Report blocked, unsuccessful and completed adaptations separately.
- Use fresh per-material timing distributions to choose bounded job sizes; expand a cell only under prespecified criteria and approved caps. Do not turn engineering qualification into the entire scientific E3 result.
- Report final independent held-out accuracy, scientific decisions, adaptation history and cost for every eligible cell, and report unchanged passing cells as passing baselines.

**Gate:** conclude the six-system scientific study only after the required coverage matrix and limitations can be reported truthfully. Never silently remove a failing combination because it is expensive or because its adaptation looks unfavorable.

### Stage 5: E4 cross-facility qualification
- Freeze one shared small characterization contract (the existing Si/SiC portability harness is the candidate), scientific tolerances, DFT method, potential files and output schema before platform execution.

- Run the same fresh, bounded scientific contract on Perlmutter, Frontier and Aurora after environment/launcher smoke checks. Capture repository commit, software stack, physical and logical accelerator counts, numerical outputs, convergence, stage timings, parent allocations and retries. Prior Perlmutter artifacts are engineering-only and do not count as a facility's campaign result.

- Report one end-to-end qualification per facility as portability evidence; do not claim robust comparative performance from one timing observation. Repeat timing workloads only if performance comparison is a stated result.

**Gate:** a launcher build or smoke test is not successful scientific characterization. Full workflow, DFT-only and MLIP-only support are separate statuses.
