## Decision Summary

The evaluation shall include **all six material systems** across E1, E2, and E3: LiFePO4, Cantor FCC alloy, NbTaVHfZrTi BCC alloy, phosphorene, Zn(HCOO)2 metal–organic framework, and Nb-Ta-O. Nb-Ta-O E3 assesses candidate phases against a bounded, explicitly versioned set of elemental, binary, and ternary VASP references. Historical data may inform provenance review or candidate-geometry discovery, but no historical energies or forces are reused as E3 labels; all E3 reference labels are newly calculated with the frozen VASP signature. Candidate generation will use the internal AFLOW-prototype plus pyXtal randomized-structure mechanisms. Missing evidence must be reported, not replaced by synthetic labels or silently excluded. E3 model coverage remains conditional on backend support and valid independent evidence.

**E3 is one conditional, end-to-end experiment, not a two-material policy experiment followed by an unrelated fine-tuning study.** Build a VASP-reference dataset for all six systems, including fresh Nb-Ta-O target/reference and independent-test evidence. Evaluate pretrained HydraGNN, UMA, and MACE on every supported system–model combination (up to 18). Using predeclared, *validation-only*, task-specific acceptance criteria, identify every supported combination with insufficient predictive accuracy. Retain pretrained models that pass. For **every failing supported combination**, execute the unified E3 adaptive workflow: matched-budget comparison of LLM-guided policy C against deterministic policy D, conditional sequential VASP acquisition, and fine-tuning only when enough independent training data exist and adaptation is justified. Evaluate resulting models on independent test data and assess whether the scientific characterization requirements are met. Do not select failing combinations from final test results. A model incapable of serving a system is explicitly 'unsupported', not a passing or failing accuracy result. Eighteen initial evaluations do not imply eighteen fine-tuning campaigns.

Distinguish (i) inadequate MLIP predictive accuracy, which can motivate adaptation, from (ii) insufficient scientific evidence, which can require DFT even when the MLIP passes accuracy checks. Both decisions are governed by predefined contracts and do not authorize unsupported hull-stability claims. For Nb-Ta-O, generate all candidate structures, reference phases, labels, and validation/test evidence anew. Generate and validate only evidence required for six-system E2 and E3 endpoints. Any irreducible coverage gaps must narrow manuscript claims explicitly.

Use a staged sequence. Freeze the scientific protocols and reference methods, then generate the six-system DFT evidence required under explicit, approved resource limits. E1 can proceed without DFT; E2 uses independently reviewed evidence, and E3 establishes leakage-aware reference splits before evaluating up to 18 supported MLIP–system combinations. Qualify the numerical pathway on one or two representative cases, **but do not treat this small engineering qualification as the scientific scope of E3**. Authorize matched C-versus-D adaptive execution only for supported combinations that fail prespecified accuracy criteria, subject to a prespecified budget and stopping rule. E4 remains a single representative scientific workload qualified across three facilities, not a six-system × three-facility Cartesian product.

Retained Si/SiC characterization remains engineering evidence only. Recovered Nb-Ta-O QE artifacts are not campaign outputs and are not reusable as E3 labels; preserve them separately and do not substitute them for VASP hull references or independent test groups. Manuscript-era five-system datasets, warm-start outcomes, and aggregate totals are excluded from this campaign's baseline unless their archives are recovered and pass a separately documented provenance, method-compatibility, split-leakage, and accounting review; even then, they do not become E3 VASP labels. Do not present historical results as outputs of the new campaign. No pilot or production jobs are authorized by this plan.

### Nb-Ta-O Audit and Proposed Bounded Domain

**Historical QE run; excluded from E3 labels.** The run at `/global/cfs/cdirs/m5216/mlupopa/runs/portability/nb-ta-o--7llm-uma-al-qe-hull--653eb06--j59874625` used QE 7.6-dev, PBE, Nb/O pseudopotentials, 80/640 Ry cutoffs, Gaussian smearing, and four A100 GPUs per one-node QE child. The dataset manifests declare backend `qe`, method signature `qe-9b7290c69805cc1a`, raw QE total energy, and SHA-256 hashes for training and validation files. Eight training and two validation frames carry energies and forces. These are historical QE records, not E3 VASP labels; they are excluded from E3 train/validation/test sets, thresholds, rankings, and hulls regardless of any file/hash audit. They are not ten independent phase relaxations and cannot be used as hull energies. The validation frames are not an independent test set. The reference manifest lists structures for Nb, Ta, O2 and eleven competing formulas, but no computed compatible reference energies or convergence records; none of those entries currently qualifies as a hull reference.

**Target proposal for author review.** Make NbTaO4 the primary target composition, represented as the x=0.5 point of the rutile-derived `(Nb,Ta)O2` solid-solution line. Compare rutile-related and CaCl2-related parent structures and a small set of symmetry-distinct Nb/Ta orderings. Do not assume an ordered NbTaO4 line compound: report ordering energy relative to the lowest disordered/supercell approximant, and if no ordering is resolved call it a solid-solution composition, not a discovered ordered phase. Keep NbTaO5 as a secondary, explicitly lower-confidence block/T-Nb2O5-derived candidate. It is exploratory and must not become a required stable phase solely because an LLM proposed it. Exclude reduced shear formulas such as Nb12O29 from mandatory scope until a crystallographic source and formula/prototype mapping are independently validated; treat them as a later scope expansion.

**Minimum declared competitor domain (proposal, not exhaustive chemistry).** Include elemental Nb, Ta, O2; the Nb-Ta binary at NbTa plus elemental endpoints; Nb-O formulas NbO, NbO2, Nb2O3, Nb2O5; Ta-O formulas TaO, TaO2, Ta2O3, Ta2O5; and ternary target compositions NbTaO4 and NbTaO5. For each formula, freeze explicit phase IDs before calculation: curated structures where available, at least two distinct plausible polymorphs for NbO2, Nb2O5, TaO2, and Ta2O5, and the declared AFLOW/order variants for the ternary targets. Other formulas require at least one validated candidate phase ID. A formula is a composition requirement, not evidence that one prototype exhausts its polymorphs. This scope supports only the manuscript wording “stability relative to the declared DFT reference domain” or “domain-qualified convex-hull assessment.” List omitted ternary compositions, disordered configurations, high-pressure phases, and finite-temperature effects as out of domain.

**Three independent gates.** (1) Formula gate: the actual composition parsed from each structure matches its declared formula/phase record, and every required formula/subsystem is represented. (2) Polymorph gate: every predeclared phase ID and minimum polymorph family is present, hash-stable, and evaluated; store missing or undersampled families separately from formula gaps. (3) Convergence/compatibility gate: each required energy is from a converged geometry relaxation under the frozen VASP signature, POTCAR/correction policy, and convergence tolerances; SCF convergence or `JOB DONE` alone is not geometry convergence. Set `domain_qualified=true` only when all three gates and elemental references pass. Otherwise set `provisional=true`, `domain_qualified=false`, and expose the exact missing formula IDs, polymorph IDs/groups, and unconverged/incompatible records. A qualified hull remains relative to the declared domain, not a global completeness claim.

**E1-E3 roles remain distinct.** E1 proposes targets and tests (including the solid-solution alternative), without treating model consensus as evidence. E2 interprets a frozen, audited evidence packet and must identify historical versus newly generated records. E3 evaluates supported pretrained MLIPs, applies the predeclared accuracy gate, performs matched C-versus-D adaptive acquisition for failing cells, and independently completes the DFT evidence required for the Nb-Ta-O domain hull even if an MLIP passes. A hull requirement is not an excuse to adapt a passing model; a passing model is not a substitute for missing converged references.

**Cost and feasibility status.** The recovered run's QE application and allocation observations describe that historical QE workload only; they cannot be scaled or converted into VASP timings or costs. The proposed domain's structure count, VASP relaxation steps, failure/retry rate, concurrency, facility/account balance, and verified billing rules are not yet established. There is therefore no defensible numeric VASP node-hour, GPU-hour, or monetary estimate for E3. After explicit authorization, qualify representative VASP structures on the selected facility, reconcile application timers with scheduler allocation records, then estimate from the frozen manifest with separate retry/setup headroom and hard caps. No job is authorized by this cost note.

**Required software changes for a later implementation review (not implemented here).** Extend `ReferenceCompletenessPolicy` with required phase IDs and polymorph-family/minimum-count requirements; extend its report with separate formula, polymorph, and convergence/compatibility gaps plus `domain_qualified`. Keep `provisional` true whenever any declared requirement fails. Persist per-phase structure hash, parsed formula, phase family, backend/method signature, geometry convergence criteria/result, and source/campaign provenance. Validate formula correctness before hull construction, and require convergence independently of SCF success. Emit versioned hull snapshots containing the domain manifest/version, candidate phase IDs, energies-above-hull, decompositions, missing/undersampled regions, and the three gate results.
